# Garment Guidance

## Tailored Pieces

Tailored trousers, structured skirts, and similarly cleanly constructed garments generally increase an outfit's polish.

They can anchor professional combinations when paired with refined tops and structured layers, but tailored garments are not limited to workwear.

Pairing tailored bottoms with softer, fitted, expressive, or refined tops can transition them into smart-casual, dinner, date, and evening outfits.

## Denim Formality

Denim exists across a range of formality rather than occupying one fixed category.

Dark, clean, well-fitting, minimally distressed jeans generally appear more polished than faded, distressed, oversized, or highly relaxed denim.

Polished denim may work for casual, smart-casual, and some business-casual contexts. Relaxed or distressed denim generally belongs toward the more casual end of the range.

## Refined Materials and Texture

Materials with a smooth, refined, or visually rich appearance, such as silk or satin, can increase the perceived dressiness of an outfit.

Texture can help a simple silhouette feel more intentional or evening-appropriate.

Material alone does not determine formality. Garment type, silhouette, coverage, surrounding pieces, and occasion remain important.

## Graphic and Strongly Casual Pieces

Graphic tees and similarly casual statement pieces generally begin on the casual end of the formality range.

Combining them with tailored bottoms or structured layers can create an intentional elevated-casual or smart-casual outfit.

This transformation does not necessarily make the garment appropriate for conservative professional environments.

## Sleeveless and Base Tops

Sleeveless garments are not inherently casual or inappropriate.

A refined sleeveless blouse may work in smart-casual and many business-casual contexts, while a simple tank or camisole may require additional styling or layering for the same setting.

Construction, neckline, coverage, material, surrounding garments, and occasion are more informative than sleeve presence alone.

## Knitwear and Relaxed Garments

Knitwear and relaxed garments can span several levels of formality depending on their construction, fit, condition, and surrounding pieces.

Refined or fitted knitwear paired with tailored bottoms can appear polished, while oversized or highly relaxed pieces paired with casual bottoms generally create a more casual effect.

Evaluate the resulting silhouette rather than assigning all knitwear or relaxed garments the same level of formality.
