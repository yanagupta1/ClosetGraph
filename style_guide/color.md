# Color and Pattern Guidance

## Color Cohesion

A cohesive outfit does not require exact color matching.

Colors can work together through neutral foundations, controlled contrast, related tones, complementary relationships, or intentional repetition.

Evaluate the palette across the complete outfit rather than rejecting combinations simply because the garments are not from the same color family.

## Neutral Foundations

Black, white, cream, grey, navy, beige, camel, brown, and similar neutrals are versatile foundations.

Multiple neutrals can work together, and neutral garments can help balance stronger colors, textures, graphics, or patterns.

Neutral colors alone do not make an outfit formal, professional, or visually interesting.

## Visual Balance

When one garment has a strong color, graphic, texture, or pattern, simpler surrounding pieces can allow it to serve as the visual focus.

This is a useful strategy rather than an absolute rule. Multiple expressive elements can work together when their colors, scale, and overall composition remain intentional.

Avoid rigid traditional color prohibitions when the actual combination is visually coherent.
